Fire needs **heat, fuel and air**. Most failed fires are short on air (packed too tight) or skip a size of fuel.

**Teepee** lights fast and throws a tall flame. Good for getting going and boiling water. Burns through fuel quickly and collapses as it burns.

**Log cabin** burns slower and makes an even bed of coals. Good for cooking. Takes a little longer to catch.

Wet weather: split wood to get at the dry inside, and gather standing dead wood rather than wood off the ground.
